Iris Regression

Does the relationship between petal and sepal length vary by species?

R · regression · model comparison · November – December 2025
Source code ↗
The species-interaction comparison gives F(2, 144) = 1.683, p = 0.1895. The fitted slopes differ, but this sample provides insufficient evidence for a species interaction at the 5% level. The November 2026 report corrects that interpretation.
